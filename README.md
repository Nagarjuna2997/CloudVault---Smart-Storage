# CloudVault - File Storage

CloudVault is a simple and private file storage app that lets you use your own private Telegram channel as a personal cloud vault. Upload, manage, and delete files directly from your iPhone without relying on third-party storage servers.

With CloudVault, your files stay under your control. Simply connect your Telegram bot once, upload your files, and manage everything from one clean app interface.

# App Store

# Features

- Upload photos, videos, PDFs, and documents from your iPhone
- Store files in your own private Telegram channel
- Browse and manage uploaded files in one organized interface
- Delete files from CloudVault and automatically remove them from Telegram
- Connect your Telegram bot once and start uploading quickly
- No third-party storage servers
- No account required inside the app
- No data collection

# How It Works

1. Create a private Telegram channel
2. Create and connect a Telegram bot
3. Open CloudVault and connect your bot
4. Upload photos, videos, PDFs, or documents
5. Manage or delete your files anytime

# Privacy

CloudVault is designed around user control and privacy.

Your files are stored in your own private Telegram channel. CloudVault does not collect user data, does not track activity, and does not use third-party storage servers for your uploaded files.

# Use Cases

CloudVault is useful for:

- Personal file backup
- Saving documents privately
- Storing photos and videos
- Managing PDFs and important files
- Keeping files accessible through Telegram infrastructure
- Creating a lightweight personal cloud vault

# Compatibility

CloudVault is available for iPhone and iPad.

Requires iOS 26.2 or later.
