## About

CloudVault is a private file storage app for iPhone and iPad that lets you use your own private Telegram channel as a personal cloud vault. Upload photos, videos, PDFs, documents, and other files directly from your device, manage them inside the app, and delete them whenever needed.

CloudVault is designed for users who want simple file storage without traditional cloud accounts, external storage servers, tracking, or data collection.

## Features

### ☁️ Telegram Cloud Vault
- Store files in your own private Telegram channel
- Use Telegram as your personal storage layer
- Keep control of where your files are stored

### 📤 File Uploads
- Upload photos
- Upload videos
- Upload PDFs
- Upload documents
- Upload other important files from your iPhone or iPad

### 🗂️ File Management
- Browse uploaded files in one clean interface
- View stored files whenever you need them
- Delete files directly from CloudVault
- Removed files are also deleted from Telegram

### 🤖 Telegram Bot Setup
- Connect your Telegram bot to CloudVault
- Add the bot to your private Telegram channel
- Upload files through your own Telegram setup

### 🔒 Privacy First
- No account required inside the app
- No analytics
- No tracking
- No third-party storage servers
- Your files stay connected to your Telegram channel

## How It Works

1. 🔐 Create a private Telegram channel
2. 🤖 Create a Telegram bot
3. 🔗 Add your bot to the private channel
4. 📱 Open CloudVault and connect your bot
5. 📤 Upload photos, videos, PDFs, or documents
6. 🗂️ Browse, manage, or delete your files anytime

## Use Cases

- 🧾 Save important documents

## Requirements

- iOS 26.2+
- iPhone or iPad
- A Telegram account
- A private Telegram channel
- A Telegram bot

## Privacy

CloudVault does not collect personal data, does not track user activity, and does not use analytics.

Your uploaded files are stored in your own private Telegram channel. CloudVault does not store your files on its own servers and does not require a separate in-app account.
