# CloudVault Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: February 14, 2026**

---

## Introduction

CloudVault ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

---

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data or files to our servers.**

CloudVault is designed with a privacy-first approach. Here is what the app accesses and how it is handled:

**Files You Upload**
All files you upload through CloudVault are stored directly in your own private storage channel. We do not have access to your files. We do not copy, store, or process your files on any server owned or operated by us.

**Credentials**
Your channel connection credentials (bot token and channel ID) are stored locally on your device using Apple's Keychain, which is encrypted and protected by your device's security. These credentials never leave your device and are never transmitted to us.

**File Metadata**
Basic file information such as file name, file size, upload date, and file type is stored locally on your device using Core Data. This metadata is used solely to display and organize your files within the app. This data is never sent to any external server.

**Camera and Photo Library Access**
If you choose to upload photos or videos, the app will request access to your camera or photo library. This access is used only to select files for upload. We do not access your camera or photo library for any other purpose.

---

## Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect any of the following:

- Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
- Location data
- Usage analytics or tracking data
- Advertising identifiers
- Browsing history
- Contacts
- Health data
- Financial information

---

## Third-Party Services

CloudVault uses the Telegram Bot API to facilitate file storage in your private channel. Your interaction with Telegram is governed by Telegram's own privacy policy, available at https://telegram.org/privacy. We encourage you to review their policy. CloudVault does not share any data with Telegram beyond what is necessary to upload, retrieve, and manage your files in your own channel.

No other third-party services, analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs are used in the app.

---

## Data Storage and Security

- All file metadata is stored locally on your device.
- All credentials are stored in Apple's encrypted Keychain.
- No data is stored on any remote server owned by us.
- All communication with the Telegram Bot API occurs over HTTPS encryption.
- We do not have the ability to access, view, or recover your files or credentials.

---

## Data Deletion

**Deleting Files**
You can delete any file from within the app. Deleted files are permanently removed from your storage channel and cannot be recovered.

**Deleting Your Account / Disconnecting**
You can disconnect your channel at any time from the Settings screen. This clears all locally stored metadata and credentials from your device. Your files will remain in your Telegram channel unless you delete the channel separately.

**Deleting the App**
Uninstalling CloudVault removes all locally stored data, metadata, and credentials from your device. Your files will remain in your Telegram channel unless you delete it separately.

**Channel Deletion**
If you delete your Telegram channel, all files stored in that channel are permanently lost. CloudVault cannot recover deleted files.

## Children's Privacy

CloudVault is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

## Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store any personal data on our servers, there is no personal data for us to provide, modify, or delete. All your data remains on your device and in your private Telegram channel, fully under your control.

You have the right to:

- Delete any or all files through the app at any time.
- Disconnect your channel and clear all local data at any time.
- Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored information.
