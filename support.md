# CloudVault Support

Welcome to CloudVault support. Below you'll find answers to common questions and troubleshooting steps.

---

## Getting Started

CloudVault requires a one-time setup to connect your private storage channel. The app walks you through every step during onboarding. Once connected, you can start uploading and organizing your files immediately.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How does CloudVault work?**
CloudVault provides you with free unlimited cloud storage. You upload files through the app, and they are securely stored and automatically organized into categories like PDFs, Images, Videos, Audio, Documents, and Archives.

**Is CloudVault really free?**
Yes. CloudVault is completely free with no subscriptions, no hidden fees, and no storage limits.

**What file types are supported?**
CloudVault supports all common file types including PDFs, images (JPG, PNG, HEIC, GIF, WebP), videos (MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV), audio (MP3, WAV, AAC, M4A, FLAC), documents (DOC, DOCX, TXT, XLS, XLSX, PPT, PPTX, CSV), and archives (ZIP, RAR, 7Z).

**Is there a file size limit?**
Individual files can be up to 50MB per upload.

**Can I play videos and audio inside the app?**
Yes. Videos play directly inside the app. Audio files have a built-in player with play, pause, and progress controls. Images open in a full-screen viewer with pinch-to-zoom. PDFs render natively inside the app.

**Can I share files from CloudVault?**
Yes. Tap the share button on any file to share it to other apps, messages, email, or AirDrop. Only the selected file is shared.

**Where is my data stored?**
Your files are stored in your own private channel. CloudVault does not store your files on any external server. Your data stays under your control.

**Is my data secure?**
Yes. Your credentials are stored securely in your device's Keychain. Files remain in your private channel and are not accessible to anyone else.

## Troubleshooting

**Files are not loading**
Make sure you have an active internet connection. Try pulling down on the home screen to refresh. If the issue persists, go to Settings and try disconnecting and reconnecting your channel.

**Upload failed**
Check that your file is under 50MB. Ensure you have a stable internet connection. If the upload fails, a retry button will appear — tap it to try again.

**Channel disconnected or deleted**
If your storage channel is deleted, all files in that channel are permanently lost and cannot be recovered. The app will show a notification and return you to the setup screen. You can connect a new channel to start fresh, but previous files cannot be restored.

**App is running slow**
Try clearing the cache in Settings. This removes temporary files stored on your device without affecting your uploaded files.

**Cannot connect during setup**
Make sure you have entered the correct credentials during the setup process. Double-check each step in the onboarding guide. Ensure your internet connection is stable and try again.

---

## Important Reminders

- Do not delete your storage channel. Deleting it will permanently remove all your files.
- Files uploaded outside of the CloudVault app will not appear in the app.
- Only files uploaded through CloudVault are managed and organized by the app.
